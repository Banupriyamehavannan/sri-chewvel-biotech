How we grow your plants

Four reasons growers choose tissue-cultured plantlets over field-collected suckers — each one built to remove a specific risk from your planting material.

Disease-free culture

Propagation starts from sterile tissue, not field suckers — so plantlets leave the lab free of Fusarium wilt and other soil-borne infections common to traditional planting material.

True-to-type cloning

Each plantlet is a genetic clone of a selected high-yield mother plant, so your field matures on a predictable schedule with even fruit size across every row.

Hardened for the field

Plants leave our polyhouse at roughly 30 cm tall with an established root system, already acclimatized — which shortens the time to establishment after planting.

Supply on your schedule

Two production units let us fulfil large commercial orders year-round, without the seasonal gaps that limit sucker-based nurseries.

About Sri Chewvel Biotech

Sri Chewvel Biotech is a plant tissue culture company founded by Mr. P. Deivasigamani, who has spent over ten years in high-volume plant production and supply.

Our head office and main laboratory are in Hosur, Krishnagiri District, Tamil Nadu, with a branch office and second production unit in Mathithoppu — giving us the reach to serve large orders across the region.

We follow strict aseptic protocols and grow every plantlet in a nutrient-rich medium under controlled lab conditions, so multiplication stays fast, disease-free and genetically uniform.

What we hold ourselves to

  • Documented quality checks at every stage of the lab and hardening process.
  • A team led by microbiologists and plant tissue culture scientists.
  • 4 million plants a year today, with expansion underway across both units.
